SCIENTIFIC NOTE
---------------

The L0-L5 categories are conceptual analytical layers,
not demonstrated historical stages of abiogenesis.

Network connectivity demonstrates chemical similarity/
structural organization only and does not by itself establish
historical evolutionary ancestry or a chronological pathway.

PERMUTATION VALIDATION
----------------------

Observed cross-layer network fraction = 55.10%
Random-label null expectation = approximately 77.05%

Therefore cross-layer connectivity was depleted relative to
random layer assignment, indicating stronger within-layer
organization than expected under random labeling.

This should NOT be described as statistical enrichment of
cross-layer connectivity.
