IMPORTANT SCIENTIFIC NOTE
-------------------------

The L0-L5 categories are conceptual analytical layers,
not demonstrated historical stages.

Molecular similarity and cross-layer connectivity
represent relationships within the analyzed chemical
space. They do not independently demonstrate historical
ancestry, abiogenesis, or a unique prebiotic pathway.

Permutation validation found that observed cross-layer
connectivity was lower than expected under random layer
assignment. Therefore, cross-layer connectivity should
NOT be described as statistically enriched.
